Karpathy Built HackerTube in About Two Hours

Frustrated with YouTube's recommendations, Andrej Karpathy used Claude Code with Opus 5.5 to build HackerTube, a website for discovering YouTube videos recommended on Hacker News.

~2 hoursReported build time
6,454Videos in the collection
150Topics covered

Claude screened Hacker News comments, removed spam, and narrowed the material to approximately 40,000 comments recommending videos. The site uses frames from the middle of videos as thumbnails, offers recommendations without an account, and makes its underlying data downloadable.

Karpathy describes directing Claude conversationally while personally handling the Google API key, server setup, and domain purchase. Read his write-up.

My Take

A small frustration can be the starting point for something useful. Karpathy combined existing videos, public discussions, and AI tools to create an experience he wanted to use.

What small annoyance in your research workflow would be fun to fix? Perhaps it is finding relevant papers, browsing results, or organizing frequently used resources. Start with a clear problem, build a small version, and see whether it helps.

How Different Can Offspring of the Same Sire Be?

We often focus on differences between families. But how much variation remains within a family?

Under a standard additive genetic model, the average within-sire phenotypic variance is at least 75% of the total population variance.

Average within-sire variance / total population variance = 1 − ¼h2 ≥ ¾

The key idea: expected offspring breeding value includes half the sire's breeding value. But halving a value scales its variance by one-quarter, not one-half.

This concerns phenotypic variance averaged across families, not allelic diversity. The model assumes additive inheritance, random mating, representative parents, and no family-specific environmental effect.
